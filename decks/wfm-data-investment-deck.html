02 · MODEL
Target operating model

One shape, both areas.

Centralize what gains from scale and consistency. Embed what needs local context. One service layer protects paying capacity — and the whole thing feeds XOOS through a single data contract.

Read it: teal = the shared center that makes capability sellable; the dashed edge is embedded per account; the dark strip is the one layer that keeps paying capacity ring-fenced.

04 · CADENCE
How it runs

A tuning loop, not a finish line.

The 14-month roadmap stands up a capability, not a one-off deliverable. Once the machine exists, WFM + Data run as a permanent loop — continuously sensing what operations and reporting need, deciding what to build or retune, and implementing it.

So this isn't a project that ends. It's an engine that keeps the data and operational-reporting infrastructure tuned as accounts, KPIs, and the AI mix keep changing. The build sets up the loop. The loop never stops.
Why it matters for the ask: you're not buying a project that finishes and depreciates — you're building a durable function that keeps paying back. The 14-month plan is how we stand it up.

07 · XOSCORE
Parallel track — starts month +3

XOScore: an objective experience score.

Rates every interaction one-by-one — score, confidence, evidence, rationale — then composes a per-brand score that calibrates over time. Run by a separate pod from month +3 (~Oct 2026): measurement- and calibration-heavy early, DE-heavy only at the backbone — and it doesn't end. After lifecycle integration it continues in its own cadence loop, on its own milestones.

Dedicated DE: 1 → 1 → 2 → 2 (peak 2). Data science + CX SME lead the early, measurement-heavy phases — outside this DE ask. The main schedule risk is data access (sources, quality, approvals), not the engineering.
08 · TOGETHER
Running both at once

Two tracks, one platform.

The timelines are complementary: XOScore is DE-light while we're still building the platform, and its DE-heavy backbone phase arrives just as the platform is ready to host it. So they integrate rather than compete.

Integration — enrich the score

  • IngestReuse the main build's bronze/silver interaction pipelines instead of new adapters.
  • KeysAlign to MDM/governance — customer, interaction, agent, brand, timestamp.
  • CalibrateOutcome signal (CSAT / NPS / churn) from the Gold layer → learned, faster calibration.
  • Feed XOOSThe score becomes a Gold entity powering XOOS experience analytics.

Combined footprint

  • Separate podA distinct 2-person pod — not added to the core team's load — sharing our platform and infra.
  • DE total12 engineers — a 10-person core (incl. the lead, a current DE) plus the XOScore pod's 2, net new.
  • LeverageBecause it rides our platform, the pod needs only 2 DEs — not the 3–4 a standalone build would.
