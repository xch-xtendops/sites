02 · MVP
What "live by EOY26" means

The Dec-2026 MVP, defined.

Deliberately thin and standardized so it works across six accounts at once: breadth now, depth in 2027. Here's exactly what's in — and out — on each side.

Data backend

Pipelines & platform
  • Connectors for a minimum viable set of sources per account (interaction + ops data).
  • Medallion on Snowflake · dbt · Airflow: bespoke bronze per source → templated silver → curated Gold KPI marts.
  • Governed, standard KPI definitions applied identically across all 6 accounts.
  • Join-ready keys — customer · interaction · agent · brand · timestamp.
  • Daily batch refresh, orchestration, basic monitoring; account partitioning for isolation.
Not in the MVP: streaming, full security/compliance hardening, re-scoring infra, per-brand custom models, and the employee lifecycle model.

Data frontend

What lands in XOOS
  • One standard daily operational dashboard template, applied per account.
  • Core operational KPIs — volume, service level, AHT, occupancy, adherence/shrinkage, resolution — in a consistent layout.
  • Account-scoped access (RBAC groundwork: each tenant sees only its own).
  • Date / channel / queue filters and trend lines.
  • Consistent enough to onboard the next account by configuration, not rebuild.
Not in the MVP: journey/funnel views, AI-performance dashboards, the business-review builder, deep per-brand customization, self-serve config.
Boundary: XOOS's own app (its database, frontend, backend) is a separate team — our "frontend" is the Gold-layer data contract + the dashboard spec it renders.

04 · HIRING
DE capacity, pulled left

Onboard earlier to hit EOY26.

Six accounts by December can't wait for a Feb–Jun hiring wave. Senior platform DEs start now; pipeline DEs and bronze contractors land in Q4 2026.

  • Jul: 2–3 senior platform DEs + the PM; bronze contractors start August.
  • Sep–Oct: 3–4 pipeline DEs (pulled left from Feb) → ~7 building 6 pipelines in parallel through Q4.
  • Accounts per DE: ~1 DE per 4–6 accounts at steady state; fewer during greenfield build.
  • Ownership = build + monitor + maintain the account's pipelines and Gold entities.
  • BAU vs build: once live, maintenance is a fraction of build — freeing capacity for Phase 2 and new tenants.
The honest caveat: onboarding 4–5 DEs to productivity in one quarter is aggressive and raises spend earlier; new hires ramp before they're fully productive.
05 · WFM
Planning & Forecasting CoE

The CoE — plus an automation tool.

The WFM CoE stands up as planned (reskill, blended human + AI planning), and we add a scoped build inside its development: a forecasting / scheduling / capacity-planning tool — usable internally and sellable in XOOS.

WFM CoE development

Reskill + build
  • Central Forecasting & Planning; scheduling + RTA embedded per account under a WFM POC.
  • Reskill current staff (flat headcount) into central forecasting and blended human + AI capacity planning.
  • New: a WFM automation tool — forecasting, scheduling, capacity planning — as a scoped P&F project, then a product surfaced in XOOS.
  • WFMaaS (blended) enabled Feb 2027; the tool matures across 2027.

Headcount effect

Ratio improvement, not cuts
  • ROI shows up as WFM coverage scaling sub-linearly as agent and account headcount grow.
  • The win is avoided future hiring — absorbing growth without proportional WFM growth.
  • No current cuts, no backfill skip — we reskill existing staff to guarantee operational continuity.
  • Becomes a sellable product — clients get automated planning inside XOOS.
Scope note: this is the WFM-platform dependency we flagged, now elevated to a funded, scoped build within the CoE — with its own timeline to define.

Added to the roadmap

Three additions, sequenced on what exists.

AI ROI analytics

Mid–late 2027
  • Client-facing view of AI performance: containment, resolution, sentiment, human-vs-AI.
  • Composed from existing tracks — XOScore, QA automation, blended-capacity economics.
  • Not net-new plumbing — an assembly layer over data we already build.

Business Reviews

Late 2027
  • Turn analytics into a presentation built and delivered inside XOOS.
  • Sits on top of the analytics layer — so it follows once that layer exists.

Journey analytics

Tiered
  • Tier 1 (Q1'27): service journey on 2–3 accounts — contact reason → routing → AI/human → resolution → repeat — from our interaction data.
  • Tier 2 (later '27): full discovery→loyalty funnel — gated on client upstream data (e-commerce / CRM).
Why tiered journeys: our BPO data covers the service leg; the full funnel needs the client's own upstream data — which turns the ask into leverage to push data-sharing.
08 · VOC
Proposed third track — decision pending

Voice of Customer: what it would take.

A Perplexity-style, per-account interface that surfaces trends and topics from customer conversations. Real value — but it's a third parallel track, not a feature to fold into the current ask.

What it would mean

  • Its own pod — like XOScore: separate headcount, timeline, and ownership.
  • New pipelines: conversation ingestion across channels, transcription, PII handling, topic modeling, embeddings.
  • A RAG / search + LLM layer over the conversation corpus, per account.
  • Shares the platform and join-ready keys (some reuse from XOScore's interaction ingestion).
  • Depends on conversation-data access & quality — the recurring gating risk.

If we start October 2026

  • Oct–Dec '26: data access + conversation ingestion, transcription & normalization.
  • Q1 '27: topic/trend surfacing MVP on 1–2 accounts.
  • Q2 '27: the query UI (Perplexity-style) MVP; broaden accounts.
  • H2 '27: production, more accounts, tie-in with XOScore/journey.
  • ~9–12 months to a solid product — contingent on a dedicated pod + data access.
The decision: worth doing, but it needs its own pod and budget. Absorbing it silently would double the parallel-work load — so it's a yes/no on funding a third track, not a roadmap footnote.
