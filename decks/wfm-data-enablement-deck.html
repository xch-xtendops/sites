01 · GAP → PLAN
What it takes

What must improve, and what we'll do.

Required to improve

Today's gaps
  • Central forecasting — today it's done per account, inconsistent and unscalable.
  • A consistent roster integration process — standardized intake of operations rosters.
  • Consistent, timely attendance reporting — reliable actuals to forecast and adhere against.
  • A real Data Engineering backbone — 2 engineers and bespoke, per-account pipelines.
  • Data governance from zero — no KPI definitions, MDM, quality, or ownership.
  • BI delivery discipline — ad-hoc per account, no intake or reuse.
  • A multitenant Gold layer to feed XOOS and external consumers reliably.
  • Clear roles for a federated model — who owns what, and who develops whom.

What we propose

The move
  • Stand up two CoEs — Data (Engineering + Governance) and WFM (Forecasting + Planning).
  • Federate delivery — BI satellites; scheduling & RTA embedded under WF POCs.
  • Build the platform on Snowflake · dbt · Airflow with tenant isolation + a governed semantic layer.
  • Reskill and repurpose existing staff; hire only for the backbone (DE + a PM).
  • Run it as a permanent tuning loop — analyze → decide → implement.
  • Prove it fast on an MVP (Warby Parker + M&M), then scale.
02 · MODELS
Operating models

Two CoEs, one pattern.

Each area is a centralized center of excellence at the core, with delivery and consumers arranged as satellites around it.

Data CoE model
Centralized DE + Governance produce the platform and governed data; BI, XOOS, and external apps consume it.
WFM CoE model
Central forecasting + planning sets the standards; a WFM POC per account runs scheduling and RTA/data-harvest at the edge.
